The **most-cited and actively maintained** survey is the Agentic RAG survey (2501.09136, Singh et al.), now in its 4th revision and widely regarded as the reference taxonomy for agentic RAG. The SoK paper (2603.07379) is the newest dedicated agentic RAG survey, focusing on systemic risks and a research roadmap. For a broader (non-agentic) overview updated in 2026, the Knowledge-Oriented RAG survey (2503.10677, v3 Jul 2026) is the most comprehensive.
